Coffee in Italy: A Sip Worth the Trip!

Italy takes great pride in its espresso, and enjoying a cup is a wonderful way to experience the local culture. When I traveled to Italy for business, the people I worked with wouldn’t start the workday until they had their espresso. Before lunch, there was always another break to enjoy a shot together. These moments were a chance to slow down, connect, and enjoy a daily tradition. Even if you don’t normally drink coffee, the aroma of freshly brewed espresso or a creamy cappuccino paired with a warm pastry might tempt you to try something new. If you’re traveling for business, take time to talk with the people who live there and learn about the food and drinks they enjoy. Sharing their traditions can make your trip more memorable and help you build stronger connections.
